## Full description

Build the row. Break the line.

Brickly Break is a colorful offline block puzzle where every placement matters. Drag a piece anywhere it fits, complete horizontal rows without gaps, and watch the remaining blocks settle to the base after each break.

PLAY YOUR WAY

- Journey through 50 progressively challenging levels.
- Unlock each level and earn up to three stars for efficient play.
- Chase your personal high score in Endless Classic.
- Choose from multiple backgrounds and block skins.
- Learn quickly with a five-step first-play tutorial.

SIMPLE RULES, SMART STRATEGY

- Blocks stay exactly where you drop them.
- Complete horizontal rows to break them.
- Every broken block earns 10 points.
- Gravity activates only after a row breaks.
- Keep the stack away from the top and preserve space for future pieces.

OFFLINE AND PLAYER-FRIENDLY

Brickly Break has no account requirement, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no online gameplay requirement. Scores, settings, and Journey progress stay on your device.
